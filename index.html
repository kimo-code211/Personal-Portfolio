01 — About

A student who builds, learns and keeps improving

I'm Pursuing Bachelor of Science in Electronics Technology, student at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton, specialising in Communication Electronics. My coursework gives me a solid grounding in how signals, circuits and communication systems actually work.

Alongside my degree, I'm teaching myself programming and web development. I started with HTML and CSS, moved into basic Java, and I'm now working through JavaScript, responsive design, and Git & GitHub.

What interests me most is the point where these fields meet — using electronics knowledge together with code to build practical solutions rather than theory on paper. I'm still early in that journey, and I'm honest about it: I'm a continuous learner who prefers building small, real projects to claiming skills I don't have yet.

I'm currently looking for internship and scholarship opportunities where I can contribute, be mentored, and grow as a developer.
